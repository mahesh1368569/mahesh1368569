# Hello 👋 I'm Mahesh Chinthalapudi

### Microbiome Scientist | Bioinformatician | Computational Biologist

I am a computational microbiome researcher working at the intersection of **microbial ecology, bioinformatics, biogeochemistry, and multi-omics**.

My research focuses on understanding how environmental conditions and management practices shape microbial communities and their functional roles in **carbon, nitrogen, and other biogeochemical cycles**.

I work with microbiomes across **soil, plant, freshwater, estuarine, animal, and agricultural systems**, using amplicon sequencing, shotgun metagenomics, metatranscriptomics, ecological statistics, and machine-learning approaches.
